# Decision Log: Queen City Supply Co. Migration

| ID | Date | Decision | Reason | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| D-001 | 2026-10-29 | Create the company from the **Production – Setup data only** template (BC 28.3, US localization). | Provides number series, source codes, report selections and baseline posting setup without sample transactions; a blank company would require building all of that by hand. | Agreed |
| D-002 | 2026-10-29 | Install only these modules: Foundation, Common, Finance, Bank, Sales, Purchase, Inventory, Warehouse, CRM. | Matches the client brief. Fixed assets (G/L balances only), HR/payroll (external provider), and jobs are out of scope. CRM is needed for contacts created with customers and vendors. | Agreed |
| D-003 | 2026-10-29 | Chart of accounts: **hybrid approach**. Keep the template's structure and control accounts; rename or add accounts to match the client's QuickBooks chart; block accounts the client will not use. | Keeps posting setup working while giving the client familiar account names. QuickBooks Online charts typically have no account numbers, so BC numbering is acceptable. | Agreed |
| D-004 | 2026-10-29 | Opening balances are loaded through subledgers (sales, purchase and item journals) and a general journal for all other accounts, all against a temporary opening balance clearing account that must end at zero. | Keeps subledgers and control accounts equal, so aging and inventory valuation are correct from day one, and proves the migration balances. | Agreed |
| D-005 | 2026-10-30 | Load the year-to-date balance (1/1–9/30/2026) of every income and expense account, dated 9/30/2026, rather than rolling it into equity. | The client's fiscal year is the calendar year; BC must produce the full-year 2026 P&L without relying on QuickBooks. | Agreed |
| D-006 | 2026-10-30 | Create a MIGRATION general business posting group, used only on opening balance journals, whose Inventory Adjmt. Account is 25900 Opening Balance Clearing. | Opening stock must debit inventory and credit the clearing account, not the P&L. (A comparable demo posting through cost of goods sold was found in the Finance Analytics project.) | Agreed |
| D-007 | 2026-10-30 | Create product posting groups CHEMICALS, PAPER and EQUIPMENT, mapped in General Posting Setup to 40140, 40150 and 40160. | QuickBooks posts each item's sales to its own income account; BC determines the sales account from posting groups, so each category needs its own group. | Agreed |
| D-008 | 2026-10-30 | Load Customer Deposits as a G/L liability (22300), as in QuickBooks, not as unapplied customer payments. | Deposits are rare (large orders only); keeping them in the G/L matches the QuickBooks trial balance one-to-one. Trade-off: applying a deposit to an invoice requires a manual journal entry. Revisit if deposits become frequent. | Agreed |
| D-009 | 2026-10-30 | Set up the company credit card as a BC bank account (posting group CREDITCARD → 22510 liability), not a plain G/L account. | Allows monthly card statements to be reconciled with bank reconciliation, and card payments become simple transfers from Operating Checking. The bank account balance is negative, which is expected. | Agreed |
| D-010 | 2026-10-31 | Post payment tolerance to discount accounts: customers to 40910 Discounts Given, vendors to 68290 Purchase Discounts (the template used 40330 Interest Earned). | Writing off a small underpayment is economically a discount, not interest. Applied to both sides for consistency. Depends on General Ledger Setup "Payment Tolerance Posting" using the tolerance accounts. | Agreed |
| D-011 | 2026-11-01 | Sales tax: one jurisdiction per state and per county (local rate combined), six tax areas (NC-MECK, NC-CABARRUS, NC-GASTON, NC-UNION, SC-YORK, SC-LANCASTER), tax groups SUPPLIES and NONTAXABLE. Rates verified with NCDOR (Mecklenburg 8.25% from 7/1/2026) and SC DOR ST-500 (effective 7/1/2025). | Matches destination-based sourcing and county-level reporting. Mecklenburg's transit portion can be split later if the accountant requires it. Sourcing rules and SC 2026 rates to be confirmed by the client's tax advisor. | Agreed |
| D-012 | 2026-11-01 | Payment terms: reuse 15 DAYS, 30 DAYS, 60 DAYS; create RECEIPT (0D) and 2%10NET30 (30D, 2% if paid within 10D). | Keeps the client's terms exactly; avoids printing the template's "COD" on invoices that are due on receipt. | Agreed |
| D-013 | 2026-11-01 | Inventory Setup: Location Mandatory = Yes and Prevent Negative Inventory = Yes. G/L Setup: Payment Tolerance Warning = Yes (tolerance stays at 0.1% / $1.00). | One warehouse, so every entry must be at MAIN; blocking negative stock keeps FIFO costs reliable; tolerance write-offs (now discounts, D-010) are confirmed by the user rather than posted silently. | Agreed |
| D-014 | 2026-11-01 | Numbering: customers and vendors get generated numbers in the series' own pattern (C00010, V00010, step 10, alphabetical by QuickBooks name), recorded in cross-reference files; items keep the client's QuickBooks SKU as the BC item No. After loading, each series' Last No. Used is set to the highest imported number. | QuickBooks has no customer or vendor numbers, and those numbers are internal. Item SKUs are used outside the system (labels, pick lists, customer POs), so they must not change. Cross-references link every QuickBooks name to its BC number for loading open documents and for reconciliation. | Agreed |
| D-015 | 2026-11-02 | Delivery Charge becomes an item of Type = Service ("DELIVERY"), with a new product posting group DELIVERY mapped to 40390 Delivery Income and tax group SUPPLIES. | Users pick it like any product; it holds no stock; it posts to its own income account. Taxability of delivery charges to be confirmed by the tax advisor. | Agreed |
| D-016 | 2026-11-02 | Base unit of measure assigned by pack type: CASE, PAIL, BAG, PACK, PCS. Quantities load in the same unit QuickBooks counts them in. | Matches how the client buys and sells (pending I-010); avoids converting quantities at load. | Agreed |
| D-017 | 2026-11-06 | Opening AR/AP: Posting Date = 9/30/2026 for all opening entries; Document Date and Due Date = QuickBooks' originals; Document No. = QuickBooks number (AR) and External Document No. = vendor's invoice number (AP); open balance only; no sales tax calculated (Tax Liable = No); Reason Code MIGRATION; balancing account 25900 Opening Balance Clearing. Journal batches have no number series. | G/L matches the QuickBooks trial balance at the cutover; aging and collections work from day one; tax is already in the trial balance; every migration entry is traceable. | Agreed |
| D-018 | 2026-11-06 | Open invoices with 2% 10 Net 30 terms whose discount date is on or after 10/1/2026 keep their discount (Pmt. Discount Date and Payment Discount % set on the line); others load without a discount. | Customers may still be entitled to the discount after go-live. | Agreed |
| D-019 | 2026-11-07 | IRS 1099 2026 period: NEC-01 (Nonemployee Compensation) and MISC-01 (Rents) minimum reportable amounts changed from 600.00 to 2,000.00 (both were created with 600.00 by the IRS Forms Guide). MISC-03 Other Income and MISC-06 Medical and Health Care Payments still show 600.00; other MISC boxes unreviewed. None are used by the client except NEC-01 and MISC-01. | The 1099-NEC/MISC reporting threshold rose from $600 to $2,000 for payments made from 1/1/2026. Confirmed in trial run 2 (BC 29.0): the IRS Forms Guide still creates NEC-01 and MISC-01 with 600.00; changing them to 2,000.00 is a required runbook step on both versions. | Proposed |
| D-020 | 2026-11-07 | No item tracking (lot or serial numbers) at go-live; "Item Tracking on Lines" off on OB-STOCK. | QuickBooks Online Plus holds no lot or serial data, so there is nothing to migrate. Lot tracking for chemicals (recalls, expiry) is a possible later phase. | Agreed |
| D-021 | 2026-11-08 | Bank opening balances: one entry per bank account at 9/30/2026 against 25900 Opening Balance Clearing, then an opening bank reconciliation dated 9/30/2026 that closes each entry. Assumption: accounts reconciled at 9/30 with no outstanding items (book = statement balance). | October's first reconciliation starts from the 9/30 statement balance with no open opening entries. On a real project, outstanding checks and deposits from the client's 9/30 reconciliation report are loaded individually instead. | Agreed |
