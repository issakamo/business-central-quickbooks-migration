# Decision Log: Queen City Supply Co. Migration

| ID | Date | Decision | Reason | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| D-001 | 2026-10-29 | Create the company from the **Production – Setup data only** template (BC 28.3, US localization). | Provides number series, source codes, report selections and baseline posting setup without sample transactions; a blank company would require building all of that by hand. | Agreed |
| D-002 | 2026-10-29 | Install only these modules: Foundation, Common, Finance, Bank, Sales, Purchase, Inventory, Warehouse, CRM. | Matches the client brief. Fixed assets (G/L balances only), HR/payroll (external provider), and jobs are out of scope. CRM is needed for contacts created with customers and vendors. | Agreed |
| D-003 | 2026-10-29 | Chart of accounts: **hybrid approach**. Keep the template's structure and control accounts; rename or add accounts to match the client's QuickBooks chart; block accounts the client will not use. | Keeps posting setup working while giving the client familiar account names. QuickBooks Online charts typically have no account numbers, so BC numbering is acceptable. | Agreed |
| D-004 | 2026-10-29 | Opening balances are loaded through subledgers (sales, purchase and item journals) and a general journal for all other accounts, all against a temporary opening balance clearing account that must end at zero. | Keeps subledgers and control accounts equal, so aging and inventory valuation are correct from day one, and proves the migration balances. | Agreed |
| D-005 | 2026-10-30 | Load the year-to-date balance (1/1–9/30/2026) of every income and expense account, dated 9/30/2026, rather than rolling it into equity. | The client's fiscal year is the calendar year; BC must produce the full-year 2026 P&L without relying on QuickBooks. | Agreed |
| D-006 | 2026-10-30 | Create a MIGRATION general business posting group, used only on opening balance journals, whose Inventory Adjmt. Account is 25900 Opening Balance Clearing. | Opening stock must debit inventory and credit the clearing account, not the P&L. (A comparable demo posting through cost of goods sold was found in the Finance Analytics project.) | Agreed |
| D-007 | 2026-10-30 | Create product posting groups CHEMICALS, PAPER and EQUIPMENT, mapped in General Posting Setup to 40140, 40150 and 40160. | QuickBooks posts each item's sales to its own income account; BC determines the sales account from posting groups, so each category needs its own group. | Agreed |
| D-008 | 2026-10-30 | Load Customer Deposits as a G/L liability (22300), as in QuickBooks, not as unapplied customer payments. | Deposits are rare (large orders only); keeping them in the G/L matches the QuickBooks trial balance one-to-one. Trade-off: applying a deposit to an invoice requires a manual journal entry. Revisit if deposits become frequent. | Agreed |
| D-009 | 2026-10-30 | Set up the company credit card as a BC bank account (posting group CREDITCARD → 22510 liability), not a plain G/L account. | Allows monthly card statements to be reconciled with bank reconciliation, and card payments become simple transfers from Operating Checking. The bank account balance is negative, which is expected. | Agreed |
| D-010 | 2026-10-31 | Post payment tolerance to discount accounts: customers to 40910 Discounts Given, vendors to 68290 Purchase Discounts (the template used 40330 Interest Earned). | Writing off a small underpayment is economically a discount, not interest. Applied to both sides for consistency. Depends on General Ledger Setup "Payment Tolerance Posting" using the tolerance accounts. | Agreed |
